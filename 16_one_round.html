TARA · RL week 1 · interactive 16 · one round of learning

Play, score, nudge, play again

A real CartPole and a tiny policy: π(right | s) = σ(w · s), four weights, starting at 50 / 50. One round = play 8 games, score them, nudge the weights, then play again with the new policy.

Round 1

Click 1 · play 8 games. Each strip is one game: one cell per step, teal = pushed right, grey = pushed left. Its length is the return G.

The policy in two test states

What to notice

Nobody tells the agent which push was right. It only sees which games went long. Pushes from long games get more likely, pushes from short games less likely, and the next 8 games get longer.
