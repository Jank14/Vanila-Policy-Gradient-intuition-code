Why probes

If CartPole doesn't learn, the bug could be in any of 9 functions. A probe is a world so small that the right answer is known — so a failure points at one piece.

One-step games

Every episode is a single step (done = True every time), so G = r. Probes test the sign of the loss, the log-probs and the update — not compute_returns.

Tick the bug

With −joy the optimiser maximises the wrong thing: the policy moves away from the rewarded action and the probe fails.

Watch joy in the log

It hovers near 0 and doesn't track performance — it's a surrogate whose only job is its gradient. Once every env hits 500, each column's returns are identical, the baseline cancels them, and joy (and the gradient) is exactly 0: nothing left to learn.

The policy map

Teal = push right, purple = push left. The trained net pushes toward where the pole is going: mostly by angular velocity, with the boundary tilted by the angle — the "angle + velocity" rule from Lab 1, found from +1s alone.
